# Project Brief

## Business description

Nexova Solutions is a Valencia-based human resources consulting and talent acquisition firm with an expansion office in Miami. Its 120-person business provides executive and mid-management headhunting, customer support outsourcing for technology companies, and corporate training in soft skills and leadership.

The company has strong domain expertise and established client and candidate networks, but its operations depend on disconnected legacy tools, spreadsheets, email, and manual reporting. The project will build the systems and AI capabilities Nexova needs to operate at greater scale while preserving the quality of its human-led services.

## Project objectives

- Improve sales execution and lead qualification for the 18-person Sales and Business Development team.
- Create a consistent pipeline from prospecting through follow-up, qualification, proposal, and close or discard.
- Increase CRM adoption and give managers visibility into pipeline health, inactive deals, and conversion likelihood.
- Use AI to suggest qualification signals and personalized proposal angles based on a prospect's profile.
- Improve outsourced Customer Support performance for Nexova's technology, retail, and finance clients.
- Centralize support knowledge and enable semantic search and RAG-powered first-line assistance.
- Give supervisors real-time visibility into workload, backlog, SLA risk, and ticket sentiment.
- Reduce average resolution time from 48 hours toward the committed 24-hour SLA and identify dissatisfied clients before escalation.

## Problems being solved

### Sales and lead qualification

SDRs prospect manually on LinkedIn, export lists to Excel, and send follow-ups without automation. HubSpot is used inconsistently, with only about 40% of the team updating it reliably. Follow-up falls through the cracks, deals are lost without a clear reason, and the team lacks reliable visibility into which prospects are qualified or most likely to close.

The target solution is a sales pipeline dashboard, automated prospecting sequences, inactive-conversation alerts, and AI-assisted qualification and proposal guidance.

### Customer Support

Thirty support agents handle phone, email, and web-chat incidents for outsourced clients. Knowledge is scattered across agent experience and a shared Word document in Google Drive. The average resolution time is 48 hours against a 24-hour SLA, while supervisors lack real-time workload and backlog visibility.

The target solution is a centralized knowledge base with semantic search, a RAG-based first-line support chatbot capable of resolving a meaningful share of routine queries, a real-time support dashboard, and sentiment analysis that surfaces dissatisfaction early.

## Success direction

Nexova should be able to qualify and progress leads consistently, reduce avoidable sales losses caused by missed follow-up, resolve more routine support queries without intervention, and manage SLA risk with current operational data instead of delayed manual reports.
