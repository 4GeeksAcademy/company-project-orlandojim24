# Company Choice: Nexova Solutions

## Decision

I choose **Nexova Solutions** as my company for this AI Engineering bootcamp project.

## Justification

Nexova's sales and support operations present the exact automation challenges I want to master: lead qualification, pipeline management, and intelligent customer communication at scale. The company struggles with manual prospecting, inconsistent lead scoring, and repetitive customer inquiries — all problems that demand AI solutions I'm excited to build. By automating Nexova's lead qualification pipeline and deploying a RAG-based support chatbot, I will develop core AI Engineering skills in data classification, semantic search, and multi-channel automation. This project directly addresses real operational bottlenecks and delivers measurable business impact, making it the ideal context for my portfolio.

## Departments of Interest

1. **Sales and Business Development** — Building the lead scoring and pipeline automation system.
2. **Customer Support (Outsourced Services)** — Designing the AI chatbot that resolves customer queries automatically.

## AI Automation Challenge

**Intelligent Lead Qualification and Follow-up Automation** — Build an AI system that receives incoming leads from multiple channels, automatically qualifies them based on engagement patterns and company signals, assigns a confidence score (hot/warm/cold), and triggers intelligent follow-ups for medium-confidence prospects while routing high-confidence leads to sales immediately. This requires skills in lead classification, scoring models, and workflow automation.

## My AI Agent Idea

**Intelligent Lead Qualification and Follow-up Agent**

**What it does:**
The agent receives incoming leads from multiple channels (web form, email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp). It extracts key information (company name, industry, budget signals, urgency, contact details) and automatically scores each lead on a 0-100 scale based on:

- Company fit (size, industry, match with Nexova's services)
- Engagement signals (response speed, message quality, specificity of inquiry)
- Budget indicators (explicit or implicit price sensitivity)
- Timeline urgency (when they need the service)

**What it needs:**

- Lead data from inbound channels (form submissions, emails, LinkedIn messages)
- Nexova's service offerings and ideal customer profile
- Historical data on which leads converted (to train the scoring model)
- CRM access to check if the prospect already exists

**What it produces:**

- Lead confidence score (Hot/Warm/Cold)
- Automated routing: Hot leads → immediate sales notification + priority flag
- Warm leads → scheduled follow-up email at optimal time
- Cold leads → added to nurture sequence
- Dashboard showing lead pipeline by stage and confidence level
- Sales team gets one daily summary report of qualified leads ready to contact

**Why it matters:**
Currently, SDRs manually qualify leads and lose track of follow-ups. This agent automates the triage, ensures no lead falls through cracks, and gives sales time to focus on closing instead of qualifying.
